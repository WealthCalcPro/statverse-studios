FAQ

Questions, answered clearly.

Simple information before you start.

What does ₹49 include?

Starter includes a one-page responsive website, premium custom styling, your provided branding/content, core CTA integration, launch & deployment setup and a basic SEO-ready structure.

Will I get a live website?

Yes. Once the agreed scope is complete, the site is prepared for a live launch and you receive the production URL. A custom domain is separate if you choose to buy one.

Can I use my own domain?

Yes. You can connect a domain you already own. Domain registration and any third-party paid services are separate costs.

Can you build from my idea?

Yes. Share your idea, references, content and preferred style. We turn it into a focused website scope before development.

How does the email enquiry work?

The form prepares a Gmail compose window addressed to statverse45@gmail.com. The website does not silently send mail; you review the message and press Send.

Can I request extra features?

Yes, but extra sections, advanced interactions and third-party functionality may move the project outside the ₹49 / ₹99 scope. Added work and price are confirmed before building it.
