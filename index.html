FAQ

Questions, answered clearly.

What does ₹49 include?

Starter includes a one-page responsive website, premium custom styling, your provided branding/content, core CTA integration, Vercel deployment and a basic SEO-ready structure.

Will I get a live website?

Yes. Once the agreed scope is complete, the site can be deployed to a live Vercel URL. A custom domain is separate if you choose to buy one.

Can I use my own domain?

Yes. You can connect a domain you already own. Domain registration and any third-party paid services are separate costs.

Can you build a website from my idea?

Yes. Share your idea, references, content and preferred style. We turn that into a focused website scope before development.

How long does development take?

Timing depends on the scope and how quickly the required content is provided. The delivery expectation is confirmed before work starts.

Can I request additional features?

Yes, but extra sections, advanced interactions and third-party functionality may move the project outside the ₹49 / ₹99 scope. We confirm the added work and price before building it.
